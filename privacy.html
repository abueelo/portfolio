[ the short version ]

This site keeps basic statistics about how it gets used. There are no cookies, nothing is stored in your browser, your IP address is never written down, and none of it goes to anyone else.

[ what is recorded ]

  • where the country, region and city your request came through, and the network it came from
  • what which page you opened, which sections you scrolled through and roughly how long each was on screen
  • clicks project write-ups you opened, links you followed off the site, whether you downloaded the CV
  • browser the browser string your browser sends, your window size, and a rough guess at whether the visit came from a person or a crawler

[ what is not ]

No cookies and nothing in local storage — the id that groups one visit together is a random number held in memory, and it's gone the moment you close the tab.

Your IP address is never stored. It goes through a one-way hash, and the result is only useful for telling one visitor apart from another. The hash is rebuilt from scratch every 30 days, so it can't follow you for longer than that.

No third-party analytics, no advertising, no tracking across other sites, and nothing is shared or sold.

[ how long ]

Visit records are deleted automatically after 90 days.

[ opting out ]

If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, nothing about your visit is recorded at all. Blocking JavaScript has much the same effect.

If you'd like anything removed, email me — the address is on the home page.
